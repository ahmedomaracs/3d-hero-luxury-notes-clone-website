Original perfume decants in Egypt

Luxury Notes sells original perfume in the size you actually need. Every decant is poured in Cairo, when you order, from a full bottle we bought and opened ourselves: 4, 6 or 10 ml in a glass atomiser. No clones, no “inspired by”, nothing diluted.

Choose from 80 fragrances by 36 houses: niche perfumes such as Amouage, Xerjoff and Maison Crivelli, many as the house’s own sample vial, and designer perfumes such as Dior, Tom Ford and Guerlain as decants. When an open bottle is down to 60% or less it moves to partial bottles, sold whole for 20% less than the same millilitres as decants.

Delivery and payment

J&T Express delivers to every governorate, usually in two to four days; the fee depends on where you are and is shown at checkout. Pay by InstaPay or mobile wallet: you get your order number straight away, and we confirm as soon as the transfer arrives.

Why a decant

A 10 ml decant is around a hundred sprays, weeks of wear, for a fraction of the bottle’s price. Live with a fragrance before you buy the bottle, or own five instead of one. New to decants? Read what a perfume decant is and how to buy one safely, or the questions people ask us.
